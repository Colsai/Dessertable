# DessertAble 🍰

**Stop scrolling. Start eating.**

DessertAble finds the best dessert spots near you in seconds. No endless lists. No decision fatigue. Just the top picks, instantly.

---

## What You Get

Enter your address. Get 5 curated dessert destinations with everything you need to know:

- **AI-Generated Descriptions** - Smart summaries of what makes each place special
- **Real Ratings** - Google reviews and scores
- **Open Now Status** - See hours and current availability
- **Drive Time** - Know exactly how far away
- **One Click Away** - Direct links to maps and menus

---

## Why DessertAble?

Because finding great dessert shouldn't take longer than eating it.

We cut through the noise. No generic restaurant listings. No overwhelming choices. Just dessert-focused results ranked by quality, distance, and what people actually love about them.

Your searches are saved. Browse your history. Find that bakery you loved last month without searching again.

---

## Get Started

**Windows:** Run `START_APP.bat`
**Mac/Linux:** Run `./START_APP.sh`

Add your Google Places API key (free tier included). Start searching.

That's it.

---
